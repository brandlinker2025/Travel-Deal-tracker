Lowest-Fare Playbook

Legitimate methods — no risky booking bypass
1. Flexible date check
Compare ±3 days and nearby months. A weekday shift can matter more than a coupon.
2. Compare final payable
Compare airline direct, Google Flights/Skyscanner and Bangladesh OTAs after taxes + payment fees.
3. Stack only allowed offers
Use bank/wallet/OTA promotions only when the campaign permits combining them.
4. Check alternate airports
For large countries, compare nearby international airports and include ground-transfer cost.
5. Split tickets carefully
Two one-way tickets can be cheaper, but baggage, missed-connection and visa risks must be priced in.
6. Avoid “hidden-city” tricks
They can violate airline conditions and create baggage/return-ticket problems. This app does not recommend them.
